Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: 9 November 2026

To request deletion of personal information processed through the ProZone WhatsApp helpdesk integration, follow these steps:

1. Submit your request

Email office@prozone-me.com with the subject WhatsApp Data Deletion Request, or use the contact information on ProZone's official website. Include the WhatsApp phone number associated with the service and request deletion of your WhatsApp integration records. Do not include identity documents or sensitive records unless requested through an appropriate secure channel.

2. Identity verification

We may ask for information necessary to confirm that the requester is authorized to make the request.

3. Review and processing

ProZone will review the request, identify the applicable integration records and respond through the supplied contact channel. Requests involving helpdesk tickets or records held by a client organization may require coordination with that organization under applicable contractual, record-retention and legal requirements.

4. Confirmation

We will communicate the outcome of the review, including any information that cannot be deleted because it must be retained under applicable obligations.

For more details about how the service handles information, see the Privacy Policy.
