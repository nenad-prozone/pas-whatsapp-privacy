Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: 9 November 2026

To request deletion of personal information associated with your use of a ProZone-operated Meta-connected application, follow the instructions below.

1. Send your request

Email office@prozone-me.com with the subject Data Deletion Request, or contact us through the details on our official website.

Identify the application you used and provide the phone number or messaging account identifier associated with your request. Do not include passwords or identity documents in your initial message.

2. Verification

We may request additional information through an appropriate secure channel to verify your identity and protect your information from unauthorized requests.

3. Processing

We will review the request and delete information under our control where appropriate. Certain information may need to be retained to satisfy legal, security or other legitimate obligations. Information held independently by Meta is subject to Meta's own privacy processes.

4. Response

We will respond through the contact details you provide and explain the outcome of your deletion request.

For more information, see our Privacy Policy.
