Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: 9 November 2026

To request deletion of personal information processed through the PAS IT HelpDesk WhatsApp integration, follow these steps:

1. Submit your request

Email nenad.pribicevic@prozone-me.com with the subject PAS WhatsApp Data Deletion Request. Include the WhatsApp phone number associated with the service and indicate that you are requesting deletion of your WhatsApp integration records. Please do not send identification documents or sensitive records unless specifically requested through an appropriate secure channel.

2. Identity verification

We may ask for information necessary to confirm that the requester is authorized to make the request.

3. Review and processing

ProZone will review the request, identify the applicable integration records and respond through the supplied contact channel. Requests involving PAS helpdesk tickets or other PAS-controlled records may be referred to PAS for handling under its contractual, record-retention and legal requirements.

4. Confirmation

We will communicate the outcome of the review, including any information that cannot be deleted because it must be retained under applicable obligations.

For more details about how the service handles information, see the Privacy Policy.
