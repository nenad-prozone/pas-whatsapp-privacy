Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

ProZone Middle East for General Trading WLL ("ProZone") respects your privacy. This policy describes how we process personal information when you interact with ProZone-operated applications and messaging integrations that use Meta services, such as the WhatsApp Business Platform.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the application, we may process your name or profile name, phone number, messaging account identifiers, messages and attachments you submit, information needed to handle your requests, and technical data such as message identifiers, timestamps and delivery status.

2. How we use information

We use this information to provide messaging and application functionality, respond to enquiries, process requests, deliver related communications, maintain service reliability, prevent misuse and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Sharing of information

Messages are processed through the relevant Meta platform and its service providers. We may also use authorized hosting and technology providers to operate our applications. We do not sell personal information. We disclose information only when needed to provide the service or when required by law.

4. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal, security and record-retention requirements. When information is no longer required, we delete or securely dispose of it as appropriate.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including access controls and secure communications, to protect information handled by our applications.

6. Your privacy choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that we hold, subject to applicable requirements. Contact ProZone at office@prozone-me.com or through our official website.

For instructions on requesting deletion, see our Data Deletion Instructions.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our applications or applicable requirements. The current policy will be available on this page.
