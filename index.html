Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

ProZone Middle East for General Trading WLL ("ProZone") provides the PAS IT HelpDesk WhatsApp integration as a contracted service for the Public Authority for Youth and Sports ("PAS") in Kuwait. This policy describes how information is handled when people use that service.

1. Information processed

Depending on the request, the service processes the WhatsApp phone number and message identifiers, conversation contents and language preference; employee verification details such as person ID and display name; service-request details, including equipment or asset information; approval responses and status notifications; and technical events needed to operate and protect the service.

2. Purpose

Information is used to verify eligible users, receive and respond to requests, create and update PAS service tickets in IBM Control Desk/Maximo, route approvals and status notifications, and support troubleshooting, reliability and security.

3. Sharing and service providers

Relevant request information is made available to PAS through its helpdesk systems. WhatsApp messages are transmitted using the WhatsApp Business Platform operated by Meta. Service infrastructure and authorized personnel process information only as needed to provide and support this contracted service. The integration is not intended for advertising or the sale of personal information.

4. Retention

Conversation records and technical logs are retained only as needed for service operation, support, security, contractual commitments and applicable legal requirements. Service tickets and associated records held in PAS systems are subject to PAS record-management requirements. Retention and deletion of those records may require coordination with PAS.

5. Security

ProZone applies access controls and secure communications to protect the integration. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your requests

To ask about information associated with your WhatsApp interaction, request a correction, or seek deletion, contact nenad.pribicevic@prozone-me.com. We may verify your identity and coordinate requests affecting PAS service records with PAS.

For the deletion process, see the Data Deletion Instructions.

7. Updates

This page may be updated as the service, contractual requirements or applicable obligations change. The current version will be available at this URL.
